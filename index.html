Check a Certificate, Chain, or .ovpn File

Paste one or more PEM certificates, or a whole OpenVPN profile. Each certificate gets its own card: who it is for, who issued it, when it expires, the names it covers, and its fingerprints.

Runs entirely in this browser tab. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere or saved.

Results update as you paste. Private keys are detected and flagged, never read or shown.
